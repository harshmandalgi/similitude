\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A miniature of an agent workflow can make the same decisions as the full evaluation, at a fraction
of its cost. In our study, the best miniatures kept 90--100\% of the clear decisions. Simulated
tools work when a model can know the answers, and grounded simulators also keep the diagnosis.
Replay and fewer tasks cost one fifth of a full run, with little loss. The noise ceiling shows
when the full evaluation itself is noise.

The next step is to use validated miniatures inside optimisation loops that test hundreds of
candidates. The full workflow then tests only the few candidates that survive.
