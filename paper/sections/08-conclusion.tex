\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A miniature of an agent workflow can make the same decisions as the full evaluation. In our study,
simulated tools kept 90--100\% of the clear decisions when a model can know the answers of the
tools. The grounded recipe also kept the diagnosis best. A quarter of the tasks kept 85\% of the
clear decisions at one fifth of the cost. The noise ceiling shows when the full evaluation itself
is noise.

The next step is to use validated miniatures inside optimisation loops that test hundreds of
candidates. The full workflow then tests only the few candidates that survive.
